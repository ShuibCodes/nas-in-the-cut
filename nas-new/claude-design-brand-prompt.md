Design a brand identity kit for "Nas In The Cut", a free daily business email by Nasir Hassan (known as Nas), a founder and investor based in Dubai.

WHO IT IS FOR
- Nas built gyms from the floor up, sold nine figures in real estate and now invests in creative businesses. He runs Collective 365, a Dubai community where founders train next to fighters twice a week.
- The newsletter shows the real numbers, the real mistakes and the habits that hold up. Tagline: "Real business. No filter."
- The feeling: a private bank, a Swiss watchmaker, a tailor's label. Quiet, exact, expensive. Nothing loud, nothing trendy.

PALETTE (light on dark, use exactly these)
- Deep navy #0B1422 (primary background)
- Deeper navy #07101C
- Raised navy #111D30
- Light silver #F1F4F8 (primary mark colour)
- Silver #D6DCE4
- Muted silver #98A4B7 (secondary text)
- Hairline #22314A
No gold, no gradients, no metallic effects. The silver is a flat colour.

TYPE DIRECTION
- Wordmark: "NAS" in capitals, regular to medium weight, very widely tracked (about 0.34em), clean grotesk or refined sans. Think the letterspaced labels on a watch dial or a private bank's card.
- Secondary line: "In The Cut" in sentence case, smaller, muted silver, normal tracking.
- No italics, no serif accents, no monospace.

1. PRIMARY LOGO
- Horizontal lockup: NAS followed by "In The Cut", aligned on the baseline.
- Stacked lockup: NAS centred above "In The Cut".
- One small signature detail only: a single fine diagonal cut through the crossbar area of the A, or a hairline slice at about 20 degrees that trims one letter. It must feel like a precise tailor's cut. It must never read as a crossing out.
- Clear space: the height of the N on all sides. Minimum size: 20px tall on screen.

2. MONOGRAM
- A single "N" with the same fine cut, light silver on deep navy. This is the avatar, favicon and app icon.
- Make a simplified version with a thicker cut that stays clear at 16 x 16 pixels.

3. COLOUR VERSIONS
- Light silver on deep navy (primary)
- Deep navy on light silver
- Single colour black and single colour white

4. FAVICON AND APP ICON SET (use these exact file names, the website already references them)
- favicon.svg: monogram, light silver on deep navy, square with a small 12 on 64 corner radius
- favicon.ico: 16, 32 and 48 pixel sizes in one file
- apple-touch-icon.png: 180 x 180, solid deep navy, no transparency, mark at about 60 percent of the canvas
- icon-192.png and icon-512.png
- icon-maskable-512.png: mark inside the central 80 percent safe zone
- Show every size at 100 percent zoom to prove the cut survives at 16px

5. SOCIAL AND EMAIL ASSETS
- og.jpg, 1200 x 630: left half deep navy with the wordmark and the headline "How the money actually gets made." in light silver, light weight. Right half: slot for a portrait photo of Nas, full bleed, no border.
- Instagram profile picture, 1080 x 1080: monogram, safe inside a circle crop.
- Instagram story highlight covers, 1080 x 1920: "Deals", "Fails", "Systems", "C365", "Issues" in small tracked capitals, centred.
- Email header, 1200 x 300 (displays at 600): wordmark left, "Issue 001" right.
- X banner 1500 x 500 and LinkedIn banner 1584 x 396: deep navy, wordmark and "Real business. No filter.", mostly empty space, lower left kept clear for the profile photo.
- Instagram post template, 1080 x 1350: one large number in light silver, one line of context under it, small wordmark at the bottom.

6. BRAND SHEET
- One page: logo system, colours (HEX and RGB), type, clear space, minimum sizes and five don'ts.

RULES
- Restraint is the brand. Lots of navy, thin lines, light type, one detail.
- No gold, no glows, no gradients, no shadows, no textures, no pills or badges, no emoji.
- No crowns, lions, dollar signs, laurels, marble or other luxury clichés.
- Deliver the logo and monogram as SVG with text outlined, plus PNG exports at 1x and 2x.
- Show the assets in context: phone home screen, browser tab, Instagram profile grid and a WhatsApp link preview.
