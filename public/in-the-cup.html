Coffee with Nas

One reader. One coffee. Every month.

Every month I sit down with one reader, one to one. Face to face if you can make it in person, online if you’re anywhere else in the world.

Your coffee, your questions. Business, property, building from the ground up, or the thing you haven’t told anyone yet.

House rules

  1. Come as you are. No titles at the table.
  2. No pitching. Connections first. Business follows.
  3. Phones face down. Be in the room you’re in.
  4. Bring one real question. That’s the price of the coffee.
